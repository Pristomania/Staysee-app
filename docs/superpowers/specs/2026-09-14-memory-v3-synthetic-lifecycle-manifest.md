## Exact compact notation

The notation below is a lossless authoring format. Task 5 must expand it mechanically; no value may be inferred semantically or paraphrased.

- A scenario session `sNN` has `stepId = <scenarioId>-sNN` and `conversationId = synthetic-<scenarioId>-cNN`.
- `at` is the exact session timestamp. `m1`, `m2`, and `m3` have `createdAt` equal to `at`, `at + 1 second`, and `at + 2 seconds` respectively. `U`, `A`, and `S` mean user, assistant, and system roles.
- `I(key,kind,status,sensitivity,start,end,alternative,claim)` expands to an extraction item with those values plus `scope:"cross_conversation"` and `conversationId:null`.
- `E(key,messageId,relation,supportType,episodeKey)` expands to extraction evidence with those values, `provenanceRole:"user"`, and `mentionTime` equal to the referenced message's `createdAt`.
- Every extraction is `{run:{caseId:<stepId>,extractorVersion:"memory-v3-synthetic-lifecycle-fixture-v1"},items:[...],evidence:[...]}`. `X=[]` means empty items and evidence.
- `P(type,candidate,target)` expands to `{type,candidateLocalItemKey:candidate,targetGoldMemoryId:target}`. Create and ignore use `target=null`.
- `F=[...]` is the exact `forgetMemoryRefs` list.
- `M(id,tier,kind,status,sensitivity,start,end,alternative,firstSeenAt,updatedAt,revision,claim,[evidence refs])` is one complete expected-state item. Every evidence ref `sNN:E(...)` expands to the durable evidence row from that session with its derived conversation ID. Thus every `State=[...]` specifies every material field and every accumulated evidence row; there are no hidden defaults.
- `N=[...]` is the exact `mustNotRemember` list. Empty arrays are literal.
- JSON `null` is written as `null`. Dates and timestamps are literal strings. Evidence order is canonical by `(conversationId,sourceMessageId,relation)`.
